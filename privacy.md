# Tile by Tile — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Publisher: South Copper

Tile by Tile ("the Game") is a puzzle game. This page explains what data the Game uses and why.

## No account
The Game does not ask for your name, email address or any other contact details.

## Data stored on your device
Your progress (best run, stars, score, lives, daily map streak), unlocked skins, subscription status and settings (sound, vibration, language, theme) are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the Game.

## Anonymous gameplay data (optional)
With your permission, the Game sends anonymous gameplay data about each endless-mode stage you play: app version, stage number, map seed and difficulty, result, stars, play time, move directions and timing, restarts, remaining lives and tiles, and combo. This helps us find stages that are too hard and keep the game fair.

- It is **off until you accept** the prompt in the Game, and you can turn it off at any time in **Settings**.
- It is linked only to a random install ID created on your device. It contains no name, email, advertising ID, device model, location or contacts.
- Turning it off deletes the install ID and any unsent data on your device.
- The data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our database hosted by Supabase. As with any internet request, the server can see your IP address; it is not stored with the gameplay data.
- The data is used only to improve the Game. It is never sold, shared with advertisers or used for advertising.
- Records are deleted automatically after 12 months.

## Advertising
Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device data such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and ad interactions. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses data from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Purchases
Subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

## Game Center / Google Play Games
If you are signed in, your scores and achievements are shared with Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android) under their own privacy policies.

## Notifications
If you allow notifications, the Game schedules local reminders on your device. No data is sent for this.

## Children
The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

## Changes
Updates to this policy are posted on this page.
